## 1. 10 m lead for the blue 32A CEE socket (PCE Typ 1232, 2P+E, 6h, IP67)

### Parts and checked prices

| Part | What to ask for | Price found (VND) |
|---|---|---|
| Plug | **PCE F0232-6**: 32A, 3P (2P+E), 230V, 6h, IP67, blue. PCE is sold in VN under the Panasonic name, so it can be listed as either. Same brand as the socket. | 243,600 (shopdien.vn), 255,500 (Đặng Gia Phát, BKCS; list 365,000), 325,000 (codienhaiau, older listing) |
| Plug, alternative | **MPE MPN-0232** (VN brand), 32A 3P IP67 | listed at bkcs.vn / thietbidienmang.com, price not captured |
| Cable | **Cadivi VCmt 3×4 mm²** (round, flexible, 300/500V) | 41,000 – 50,800 /m (list price 73,861 /m) |
| Cable, heavier | **Cadivi VCmt 3×6 mm²** | not captured; expect about 1.5× the 3×4 price |

The brief estimated 60,000–100,000/m for the cable. That was too high for 3×4: real prices are around 41–51k/m after the usual dealer discount.

### Updated cost estimate (plug + 10 m cable, before the far end)

| | Low | High |
|---|---|---|
| PCE F0232-6 plug | 243,600 | 325,000 |
| 10 m VCmt 3×4 | 410,000 | 740,000 (list price) |
| **Total** | **~650,000** | **~1,065,000** (≈ US$25–41) |

The brief's range of 600k–1.3M is still about right. The top of that range now only applies if you pick 3×6 mm² cable or a costly far end.

### Technical notes
- **3×4 mm² is enough for 10 m at 32A.** Voltage drop is about 32 A × 20 m (out and back) × 4.6 mΩ/m ≈ 3 V, or about 1.3% at 230 V. Choose 3×6 only if the load pulls close to 32A for hours, or if the cable will sit coiled or in the sun.
- **VCmt is PVC-sheathed.** It is fine for temporary outdoor use, but sun and abrasion wear it down over time. For a lead that stays outside permanently, rubber cable (H07RN-F 3G4) is better. Cadivi doesn't make it, so ask for "dây cao su H07RN-F 3x4".
- **Always unroll the lead fully** when it runs near 32A.
- **Far end is still undecided** (no change from the brief):
  - *32A in-line socket (cable connector)*: ask for the PCE F2232-6 series ("ổ cắm di động 32A 3P IP67"). Price not checked; expect about the same as the plug.
  - *Household sockets*: only through a small box with a 16A RCBO (or a 16A MCB plus an RCD) in front of them. Without it, a 16A socket strip sits behind a 32A breaker. This option costs the most and needs an electrician.
